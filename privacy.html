Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Information We Collect

When you register and use the RushUp Esports Android Application, we may collect minimal necessary personal information required for match organization and prize payouts:

  • Mobile Phone Number (for OTP verification)
  • In-Game Name (IGN) & Character ID (for Free Fire custom room invitations)
  • UPI ID or Wallet Details (solely for processing cash withdrawals)

2. How We Use Your Information

Your data is strictly utilized for core gaming and account verification operations:

  • To verify identity and prevent multiple accounts or cheating.
  • To publish room codes and passwords for scheduled tournaments.
  • To execute fast UPI cash prize transfers to your account.

3. Data Protection & Security

We implement bank-grade SSL encryption and secure server infrastructure. We never sell, lease, or share your personal details with third-party advertising companies.

4. Anti-Cheat & Fair Play Monitoring

To ensure a 100% fair competitive environment, matches are monitored by human referees and automated screenshot validation engines. Players caught using hacks or unauthorized scripts will face permanent bans.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding our Privacy Policy, please reach out via our support desk on our Contact Support page.
